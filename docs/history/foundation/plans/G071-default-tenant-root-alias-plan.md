## 7. Risks, stops, budget

- **Stops (Goal):** any need to suppress signup beyond the active-root web paths, implicit membership, Host/Origin rewriting, a broader grant than §3, stored-data conversion, a public slug lookup, DB-outage treated as absence, identity-service edits (A1 stop), a second migration, or > B3. Material Plan corrections are Claude-owned and reviewed.

- **r3 stops:** any Organization-count inference, a redirect anywhere but the exact root `/` GET/HEAD, forwarding query parameters, or a redirect that starts a login → stop.
- **Budget:** B3; attempt 1 at release; a failed P0 proof or a material overrun (> 1.5× the coding estimate of a step) produces a compact Result checkpoint before continuing.
- **r4 risks.** (a) D-10 pins are executed only after merge, so a wrong expectation surfaces post-merge and costs a
  bounded repair MR (mitigation and handling: §5.4 D-10 paragraph; accepted with Codex's selection (i)). (b) Parallel
  lanes share contracts, not files: a contract mismatch shows in the integration-step selectors, and W1-2 + W3-2 are
  integrated together for that reason; a contract change needs a Plan correction, not a lane-local fix. (c) AC8 stop:
  any change to a non-test file under `core/internal/identity/**`, a provider/credentialed run or a production-routing
  change → stop. (d) The B3 attempt count is per Goal: the four lanes and their correction rounds share attempt 1, and
  the lane count adds no attempt (> 1.5× a **lane's** coding estimate also triggers the checkpoint).
