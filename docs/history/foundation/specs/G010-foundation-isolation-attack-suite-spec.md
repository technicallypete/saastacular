## Requirements

### R1 — Independent, finite attack matrix

Specify a small shared synthetic world: at least two Organizations, two sibling
Groups sharing one BusinessProfile, another Group/profile, an unlinked profile,
and objects in Organization and Group scopes with different uploaders. Include
all six Organization/Group roles, a global user with different grants in both
tenants, a Group-only binding without Organization membership, and an outsider.
Group-role subjects ordinarily also have an Organization-member binding.

Record literal expected allow/deny, exact visible row sets, permitted mutation,
external error category and provider-effect expectation per chain step, citing
the accepted capability and separate storage-action matrices. Keep required gates
and alternate allow paths explicit. Expectations must not be generated by the
evaluator, helper SQL, observed output or implementation predicates. Reuse the
accepted `rls_expected_test.go` and `objects_expected_test.go` cases rather than
copying them; add only missing chain expectations. Resolve disagreements against
canonical contracts through Codex, preserving originals. No Cartesian product.

### R2 — Integrated attack chains and controls

Each chain must cross at least two existing boundaries, with the overall suite
connecting authenticated application sessions, policy/facade, raw runtime SQL
and actual private MinIO. Required chains:

- **A — Tenant/subject substitution:** reuse one global identity's contexts and
  differing A/B roles; replace Organization, Group, profile and object IDs, then
  attempt the corresponding guarded read/write/sign/delete and unfiltered SQL.
  Forged/ended/cross-request contexts fail; names/slugs/provider/model assertions
  confer no authority. Test malformed/zero selectors separately from absent rows.
- **B — Shared-profile escalation:** read/update a legitimately shared profile,
  then attempt sibling Group/link/membership/object discovery and mutation. Compare
  Organization-only versus selected-Group context, flag true/false, member read
  versus management, unrelated/unlinked profile and cross-tenant profile IDs.
  Derivation does not grant privileged settings, policy or audit authority.
- **C — Uploader privilege bleed:** member upload and own deletion succeed only
  in the currently authorized Group. Another uploader's same-Group object,
  sibling/unselected/foreign Group and Organization mutation deny. After admin
  downgrade, own deletion may remain while others' deletion denies; removal of
  Group/Organization access or session liveness removes that remaining path.
- **D — Current facts across reuse:** retain a request/context and objects across
  committed role downgrade/removal, last-profile-path unlink, policy change,
  logout/revocation, expiry and disablement. New guarded operations observe the
  change; an independent remaining profile path stays usable in valid context.
  Exercise the same observed pooled backend across A → context-free → B and selected
  Group → Organization-only where clean reuse is permitted, including ordinary
  statement failure/rollback. Exercise cancellation and poisoned initialization
  separately: require clean reset on reuse or verified destruction/replacement
  as the accepted G008 driver/discard contract requires. Never retain an unsafe
  backend merely to prove same-backend reuse.
- **E — Staged storage and bearer residual:** revoke authority while a Put is
  between provider work and publish; it cannot publish through prior authority.
  New signing/guarded reads deny after revocation; an already issued exact-object
  GET URL may work until its short real expiry. Tampered/expired URL and anonymous
  access deny. Preserve logical-delete versus physical-delete distinction.

Use existing deterministic ordering/fault seams and paired authorized positives.
The Plan identifies which steps are new integrated cases and which existing
tests supply unchanged coverage. Do not merely concatenate isolated predecessor
suites or accept any error as proof of authorization denial.

### R3 — Architecture and security invariants

Identity is global; UUID Organization is the tenant boundary and Group the scope.
Canonical nonzero UUIDs establish identity; editable slugs/names are selectors.
Only current Organization/Group RoleBindings confer stored authority; profiles
derive rights through current same-Organization attachments and policy. Invalid
tenant selection denies all tenant/projection/shared-identity rows; a structurally
valid but data-invalid selection may retain only the established global self row.

Assert direct runtime login (`session_user` and `current_user`, attributes and
memberships), not superuser SET ROLE. Raw SQL omits application predicates while
holding the trusted actor fixed. It does not claim protection against arbitrary
valid-subject impersonation by a runtime-credential holder; preserve G008's D1
self-state residuals and G009 X1–X7 rather than declaring them new defects.
Keep ENABLE/FORCE, exact grants/invoker projections/helpers and column immutability.

The scoped S3 credential is trusted bucket-level infrastructure, not actor/tenant
authorization. Prove guarded denials before provider I/O and unchanged owned
database/byte state; do not require impossible per-user isolation from that raw
credential. Keep MinIO root credentials out of assertion containers. Signed URLs
are reusable bearer capabilities until expiry (hard maximum 300 s), and in-flight
transfers may finish. Signed downloads do not receive core digest verification.
Ordinary operations preserve optional MFA and unknown assurance; reuse accepted
verified-MFA-required denial without new enrollment or provider qualification.

### R4 — Bounded repairs, privacy and inherited risk

Tests may expose conformance bugs; repair only existing reviewed foundation
behavior under the Goal's scope and Plan/Codex correction authority. New schema,
capability/security models, routes, public MCP behavior or broad repairs escalate.
Sensitive capabilities without implemented workflows are tested at the existing
decision boundary; do not invent billing, audit or membership administration.

Record secret-safe action/scope/outcome evidence and scan allow/deny/fault paths
with accepted canaries/redaction controls. No credentials, session material,
signed URLs/queries, private payloads or raw provider errors in retained evidence.
Preserve unknown-COMMIT semantics; errors alone never prove rollback. Retain
PG-001/002/003 risks, report materially new query/locking behavior through the
existing performance register; no benchmark, tuning or fresh numeric guarantee.
